Metamorphosis

Franz Kafka

Metamorphosis is a novella that explores themes of alienation and transformation. The story follows Gregor Samsa, a traveling salesman who wakes up one morning to find himself transformed into a giant insect. The narrative delves into the psychological and social implications of this transformation.

“I cannot make you understand. I cannot make anyone understand what is happening inside me. I cannot even explain it to myself.” - Franz Kafka, Metamorphosis
