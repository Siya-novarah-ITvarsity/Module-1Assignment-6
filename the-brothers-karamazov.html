The Brothers Karamazov

Fyodor Dostoevsky

Set in Staraya Russa, The Brothers Karamazov is a philosophical novel that explores themes of faith, doubt, and morality. The story follows the lives of three brothers and their father, focusing on the complex relationships and moral dilemmas they face.

“Above all, don't lie to yourself. The man who lies to himself and listens to his own lie comes to a point that he cannot distinguish the truth within him, or around him, and so loses all respect for himself and for others. And having no respect he ceases to love.” - Fyodor dostoevsky, the brothers karamazov
