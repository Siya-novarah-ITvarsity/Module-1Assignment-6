Marcus Aurelius

Meditation is a collection of personal writings by Marcus Aurelius, a Roman Emperor and Stoic philosopher. The work explores themes of self-reflection, virtue, and the acceptance of fate. It is considered one of the most influential works in the history of philosophy.
