White Nights

Fyodor Dostoevsky

Set in St. Petersburg, White Nights is a short and emotional story about a lonely dreamer who wanders the city’s streets and meets a young woman. Told by an unknown narrator, the story makes it feel as if you are the protagonist, experiencing everything from the joys of love to the pains of heartbreak firsthand.

“I am a dreamer. I know so little of life that I just can’t help dreaming. I live only in dreams, in my dreams. I’m not myself at all when I’m awake. I’m only myself when I’m asleep.” - Fyodor dostoevsky, white nights
