In Search of Lost Time

Marcel Proust

In Search of Lost Time is a seven-volume novel. The work explores themes of memory, time, and the nature of human experience. The narrative follows the experiences of a narrator and his family as they navigate social and personal relationships in late 19th-century France.

"The real voyage of discovery consists not in seeking new landscapes, but in having new eyes." - Marcel Proust, In Search of Lost Time 001
